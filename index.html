HOW I USE AI

AI as a build partner, not a shortcut

I use AI the same amount on the thinking side as the building side. When I'm working through a problem, AI helps me structure it, research it, and pressure-test my assumptions before I write a line of code. When I'm building, I vibe-code, I describe what I need and iterate with AI in Python, C++, and C# rather than writing everything by hand from scratch.

That's how the RAG Q&A system came together: AI helped me research the right retrieval architecture, then vibe-code the LangChain and Vertex AI implementation. It's also how I approached prompt evaluation, using AI to help design the comparison framework, not just to generate outputs. The constant across every project is the same goal from the "What I Do" section above: take something complex and make it simple enough for someone else to actually use.
