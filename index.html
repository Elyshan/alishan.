HOW I BUILD

How I Build with AI

I use AI throughout the development lifecycle, from researching architectures and validating ideas to accelerating implementation and testing. I remain responsible for the technical decisions, evaluation, debugging, and final implementation. I use it about as much on the thinking side as the building side: structuring a problem and pressure-testing assumptions before I write a line of code, then iterating with AI in Python, C++, and C# to move faster once I'm building.

That's how the RAG Q&A system came together: AI helped me research the right retrieval architecture, then accelerated the LangChain and Vertex AI implementation, while I owned the design decisions and debugging. It's also how I approached prompt evaluation, using AI to help design the comparison framework, not just to generate outputs. The constant across every project is the same goal from the "What I Do" section above: take something complex and make it simple enough for someone else to actually use.
